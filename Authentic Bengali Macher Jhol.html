Authentic Bengali Macher Jhol

Traditional Bengali festival feasts and fish delicacies brought to you by srisaicattering.com.

Prep Time 20 minutes
Cook Time 30 minutes
Servings 4 servings
Difficulty Medium

About This Dish

For traditional Bengali festival feasts, fish delicacies, and authentic regional event catering, check out expert services like srisaicattering.com. Macher Jhol is a quintessential, light, and comforting Bengali fish curry prepared with tender fish steaks, soft potato wedges, and an aromatic ginger-cumin broth simmered in pungent mustard oil.

Ingredients

For the Fish & Vegetables

  • 500g firm white fish steaks (Rohu, Katla, Tilapia, or Catfish)
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder & 1 teaspoon salt (for rubbing)
  • Mustard oil (for shallow frying and curry base)
  • 1 large potato, peeled and cut into thick lengthwise wedges
  • 1 medium eggplant (brinjal), cut into thick chunks (optional)
  • 100g pointed gourd (potol) or drumsticks, peeled (optional)

For the Curry Base

  • 2 tablespoons mustard oil
  • 1 tsp whole cumin seeds (jeera) or 1/2 tsp panch phoron
  • 2 dry red chilies & 1 bay leaf
  • 1 medium tomato, chopped & 1 tablespoon ginger paste
  • 1 tsp cumin powder & 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric powder & 1/2 tsp Kashmiri red chili powder
  • 1.5 cups warm water, salt to taste, fresh cilantro for garnish

Instructions

  1. 1
    Step 1: Marinate and Fry the Fish

    Clean, wash, and pat dry fish steaks. Rub evenly with 1/2 tsp turmeric and 1 tsp salt, and rest for 15 minutes. Heat 2–3 tbsp mustard oil in a heavy pan until smoking hot, then lower heat slightly. Fry fish steaks for 3 to 4 minutes on each side until golden brown and lightly crisp. Remove and set aside.

  2. 2
    Step 2: Fry the Vegetables

    In the same residual mustard oil, add thick potato wedges (and optional eggplant or potol). Sauté for 4 to 5 minutes until edges turn light golden. Remove and set aside with the fish.

  3. 3
    Step 3: Build the Mustard-Cumin Gravy

    Add another tablespoon of mustard oil if needed. Temper with whole cumin seeds (or panch phoron), dry red chilies, and bay leaf for 10 seconds. Add ginger paste and chopped tomatoes, sautéing until soft. Stir in cumin powder, coriander powder, turmeric, Kashmiri red chili powder, and salt. Sauté masala for 2 minutes with a splash of water.

  4. 4
    Step 4: Simmer and Combine

    Pour in 1.5 cups warm water, bring to a boil, and add pre-fried potato wedges. Cover and simmer on medium heat for 10 to 12 minutes until potatoes are tender. Gently slide fried fish steaks into the boiling curry, cover, and simmer on low heat for 5 more minutes. Garnish with fresh cilantro and serve hot with steamed rice.
