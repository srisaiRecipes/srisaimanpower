Authentic Punjabi Pakoda Kadhi

Comforting North Indian comfort food spreads and traditional event catering brought to you by srisaicattering.com.

Prep Time 20 minutes
Cook Time 40 minutes
Servings 4 to 6 servings
Difficulty Medium

About This Dish

For comforting North Indian comfort food spreads and traditional event catering, check out expert services like srisaicattering.com. Punjabi Pakoda Kadhi is a soulful classic featuring deep-fried onion fritters (pakodas) soaked in a slow-simmered, tangy yogurt and gram flour broth, finished with a sizzling red chili ghee tempering.

Ingredients

For the Kadhi Yogurt-Besan Base

  • 1.5 cups sour yogurt (dahi)
  • 1/2 cup gram flour (besan) & 4 cups water
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric powder & 1 tsp Kashmiri red chili powder
  • Salt to taste & 2 tablespoons oil or ghee
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds, 1 tsp cumin seeds, 1/2 tsp fenugreek (methi)
  • 1/4 tsp asafoetida (hing), 1 sprig curry leaves, 2 dry red chilies
  • 1 tablespoon ginger-green chili paste & 1/2 tsp garam masala

For the Pakodas (Fritters) & Final Tempering

  • 1 cup gram flour (besan) & 1 medium onion, sliced
  • 1/2 tsp carom seeds (ajwain), 1/2 tsp red chili powder, 1/4 tsp turmeric
  • 1 pinch baking soda, salt to taste, water for batter, oil for frying
  • Final Tadka: 1 tbsp ghee, 1/2 tsp cumin, 1 tsp Kashmiri chili powder, pinch of hing

Instructions

  1. 1
    Step 1: Make the Pakodas (Fritters)

    Combine 1 cup besan, sliced onions, ajwain, red chili powder, turmeric, baking soda, and salt. Add water gradually to make a thick, drop-able batter. Drop small spoonfuls into hot oil and fry on medium heat until golden brown and crisp. Drain on paper towels and set aside.

  2. 2
    Step 2: Prepare the Kadhi Base

    Whisk sour yogurt and 1/2 cup besan together until smooth and lump-free. Pour in 4 cups water, turmeric, and red chili powder. Heat 2 tbsp oil/ghee in a heavy pot, temper with mustard, cumin, fenugreek seeds, hing, curry leaves, dry red chilies, and ginger-green chili paste. Pour the yogurt mixture carefully while stirring continuously, bring to a rolling boil, then reduce heat and simmer uncovered for 20–25 minutes.

  3. 3
    Step 3: Combine and Finish

    Drop fried onion pakodas into the simmering kadhi during the last 5 minutes. Stir in garam masala and salt, then turn off heat. In a small pan, heat 1 tbsp ghee, stir in 1 tsp Kashmiri red chili powder and a pinch of hing, and pour this sizzling tempering over the kadhi. Serve hot with steamed rice or phulkas.
