Authentic Mangalorean Chicken Curry

Rich coastal flavors brought to you by srisaicattering.com.

Prep Time 25 minutes
Cook Time 40 minutes
Servings 4 servings
Difficulty Medium

About This Dish

For rich coastal delicacies and authentic regional event menus, check out srisaicattering.com. Mangalorean Chicken Curry (Kori Sukka / Curry) is celebrated for its deep complexity, combining freshly roasted coastal spices with rich coconut and coconut oil.

Ingredients

For the Fresh Coconut Masala Paste

  • 1 cup grated fresh coconut
  • 6 to 8 Byadagi dry red chilies & 2-3 short spicy red chilies
  • 1 tablespoon coriander seeds & 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1/2 teaspoon fennel seeds & 1/4 tsp fenugreek seeds
  • 1/2 teaspoon black peppercorns & 1-inch cinnamon stick
  • 3 to 4 cloves & 1 small piece nutmeg/mace
  • Small ball of tamarind & 4-5 shallots, 2 cloves garlic

For the Chicken Curry

  • 1.5 lbs (700g) chicken pieces (bone-in preferred)
  • 2 tablespoons coconut oil (essential for flavor)
  • 2 large onions, finely chopped
  • 1 sprig fresh curry leaves & 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1.5 cups water & Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. 1
    Dry Roast the Spices

    In a dry skillet over medium-low heat, add dry red chilies, coriander seeds, cumin seeds, fennel seeds, fenugreek seeds, black peppercorns, cinnamon, cloves, and nutmeg/mace. Roast stirring frequently for 2 to 3 minutes until aromatic.

  2. 2
    Grind the Masala

    Transfer roasted spices to a blender along with grated fresh coconut, small shallots, garlic cloves, tamarind, and a splash of water. Grind into a smooth, thick, and vibrant red paste. Set aside.

  3. 3
    Sauté Aromatics

    Heat coconut oil in a heavy-bottomed pot or clay kadai over medium heat. Add fresh curry leaves and finely chopped onions. Sauté until onions turn golden brown and caramelized (about 6 to 8 minutes).

  4. 4
    Add Chicken and Turmeric

    Add chicken pieces and turmeric powder. Sauté on medium-high heat for 5 to 7 minutes until chicken changes color and is lightly browned on all sides.

  5. 5
    Simmer with Ground Masala

    Pour the freshly ground coconut masala paste into the pot and mix thoroughly. Sauté for 3 to 4 minutes until the raw aroma of spices fades and oil begins to separate slightly.

  6. 6
    Cook the Curry

    Pour in 1.5 cups of water, season with salt, and bring to a rolling boil. Cover, reduce heat to low, and simmer for 20 to 25 minutes until chicken is tender and cooked through.

  7. 7
    Final Adjustments and Serve

    For a semi-dry sukka style, simmer uncovered for an extra 5 minutes until gravy clings to the chicken. Turn off heat, rest for 10 minutes, and serve hot with neer dose, boiled rice, or appam.
