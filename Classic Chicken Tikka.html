Classic Chicken Tikka

Popular party starters and live barbecue counters brought to you by srisaicattering.com.

Prep Time 30 mins + marinate
Cook Time 15 minutes
Servings 4 servings
Difficulty Medium

About This Dish

For popular party starters, live barbecue counters, and expert event catering, check out srisaicattering.com. Classic Chicken Tikka features tender chunks of chicken marinated twice in spiced Greek yogurt and mustard oil, broiled or pan-seared to smoky, charred perfection.

Ingredients

For the Chicken Marinade

  • 1.5 lbs (700g) boneless, skinless chicken thighs or breasts, cubed
  • 1/2 cup thick Greek yogurt or hung curd
  • 1 tablespoon ginger paste & 1 tablespoon garlic paste
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1.5 teaspoons Kashmiri red chili powder
  • 1 tsp each: garam masala, ground cumin, ground coriander
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 tablespoon mustard oil (for authentic tandoori depth)
  • Salt to taste

For Basting & Serving

  • 2 tablespoons melted butter or ghee
  • Chaat masala (for dusting)
  • Fresh lemon wedges and onion rings
  • Mint-cilantro chutney

Instructions

  1. 1
    First Marinade (Tenderizing)

    In a large mixing bowl, combine chicken pieces with lemon juice, ginger paste, garlic paste, and salt. Toss well and let it sit for 15 to 20 minutes so the flavors penetrate the meat.

  2. 2
    Second Marinade (Flavor & Richness)

    In a separate bowl, whisk together thick yogurt, Kashmiri red chili powder, garam masala, cumin, coriander, turmeric, and mustard oil until smooth. Pour over the chicken, coating every piece thoroughly.

  3. 3
    Refrigerate

    Cover the bowl and let the chicken marinate in the refrigerator for at least 2 hours (overnight yields the best smoky, restaurant-style depth).

  4. 4
    Skewer the Chicken

    If using wooden skewers, soak them in water for 30 minutes. Thread the marinated chicken pieces tightly onto skewers, leaving a little space between each piece.

  5. 5
    Cook (Oven / Grill / Tawa Method)

    Oven/Broil: Broil on a wire rack over a baking sheet for 12–15 minutes, turning halfway and basting with melted butter.
    Stovetop: Sear in a cast-iron skillet with 1 tbsp oil on medium-high heat for 4–5 minutes per side until charred in spots.

  6. 6
    Finish and Serve

    Transfer hot chicken tikka to a plate, squeeze fresh lemon juice over the top, and dust generously with chaat masala. Serve immediately with mint-cilantro chutney and onion rings. For grand parties and catered events, coordinate options with professionals like Sri Sai Catering.
