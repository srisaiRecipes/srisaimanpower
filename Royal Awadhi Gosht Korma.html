Royal Awadhi Gosht Korma

Royal wedding menus and luxury banquet spreads brought to you by srisaicattering.com.

Prep Time 30 mins + marinate
Cook Time 1 hr 15 mins
Servings 4 servings
Difficulty Medium-Advanced

About This Dish

For royal wedding menus, luxury banquet spreads, and authentic Mughlai event catering, check out expert services like srisaicattering.com. Royal Awadhi Gosht Korma is a masterpiece of Mughlai cuisine defined by its velvety cashew-almond gravy, rich pure ghee base, caramelized onions, and subtle floral essence of kewra water.

Ingredients

For the Meat Marinade

  • 1.5 lbs (700g) mutton or lamb, cut into medium pieces (bone-in)
  • 1 cup plain yogurt (dahi), beaten smooth
  • 1 tbsp ginger paste & 1 tbsp garlic paste
  • 1 teaspoon Kashmiri red chili powder & 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
  • Salt to taste

For the Aromatic Nut Paste & Gravy Base

  • 10 to 12 raw cashews & 10 to 12 almonds (soaked and peeled)
  • 2 tablespoons desiccated coconut or melon seeds (magaj), soaked
  • 4 tablespoons pure ghee (essential for rich silkiness)
  • Whole Spices: 2 black cardamoms, 4 green cardamoms, 4-5 cloves, 1-inch cinnamon, 2 bay leaves, 1/2 tsp shahi jeera
  • 3 large onions, thinly sliced (for birista)
  • 1 tablespoon kewra water or rose water
  • 1 teaspoon warm Awadhi Garam Masala powder
  • 1.5 cups warm water or mutton stock

Instructions

  1. 1
    Step 1: Marinate the Mutton

    Mix mutton pieces with yogurt, ginger paste, garlic paste, Kashmiri red chili powder, turmeric powder, and salt. Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour (overnight yields the most tender meat).

  2. 2
    Step 2: Make the Fried Onions (Birista) and Nut Paste

    Heat 4 tablespoons of pure ghee in a heavy pot over medium heat. Fry thinly sliced onions slowly, stirring frequently, until deeply golden-brown and crisp (birista). Remove with a slotted spoon, cool on paper towels, and crush coarsely. Set residual ghee in the pot aside. Separately, grind soaked cashews, almonds, and coconut/melon seeds with a splash of water/milk into a silky-smooth paste.

  3. 3
    Step 3: Sear and Braise the Meat

    Reheat residual ghee in the pot over medium heat. Drop in whole spices (black/green cardamoms, cloves, cinnamon, bay leaves, shahi jeera) and sizzle for 15 seconds. Add marinated mutton with all marinade juices, sautéing vigorously on high heat for 8 to 10 minutes until well-seared. Stir in crushed fried onions and sauté for 3 to 4 minutes.

  4. 4
    Step 4: Simmer, Add Nut Paste, and Finish

    Pour in 1.5 cups warm water/stock, bring to a boil, cover tightly, and simmer on low for 45 to 60 minutes (or pressure cook for 5–6 whistles) until fork-tender. Pour the smooth cashew-almond-coconut paste directly into the simmering curry, stirring continuously, and simmer on low for 10 minutes until velvety. Sprinkle Awadhi garam masala and drizzle kewra water over top. Turn off heat and rest covered for 10 minutes.

  5. 5
    Serve
