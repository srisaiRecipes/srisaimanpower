Traditional Andhra Pulihora

Authentic festival spreads brought to you by srisaicattering.com.

Prep Time 15 minutes
Cook Time 25 minutes
Servings 4 servings
Difficulty Easy

About This Dish

For authentic festive spreads and traditional temple-style South Indian catering, check out srisaicattering.com. Andhra Pulihora is a celebrated tangy rice dish packed with crunchy peanuts, lentils, green chilies, and a rich tamarind tempering.

Ingredients

For the Rice

  • 1.5 cups raw long-grain rice (or Sona Masuri)
  • 1 teaspoon oil or sesame oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder & Salt to taste

For the Tempering & Crunch (Talimpu / Popu)

  • 3 tablespoons sesame oil or groundnut oil
  • 1 teaspoon mustard seeds & 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 tablespoon chana dal & 1 tbsp urad dal
  • 2 to 3 tablespoons raw peanuts (groundnuts)
  • 10 to 12 cashew nuts (optional)
  • 3 to 4 dry red chilies & 2 green chilies, slit
  • 2 sprigs fresh curry leaves & 1/4 tsp asafoetida (hing)
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder

For the Tamarind Extract

  • Lemon-sized ball of tamarind, soaked in 1/2 cup warm water
  • Salt to taste & A pinch of jaggery (optional)

Instructions

  1. 1
    Cook and Cool the Rice

    Cook the rice with slightly less water than usual so the grains remain separate, firm, and non-sticky. Spread hot rice on a wide plate, drizzle with 1 teaspoon sesame oil, 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder, and salt. Mix gently and let cool completely.

  2. 2
    Extract Tamarind Paste

    Squeeze soaked tamarind thoroughly to extract thick juice, straining out any seeds or fiber.

  3. 3
    Cook the Tamarind Base (Pulikachal)

    Heat 1 tablespoon of oil in a small pan, add tamarind extract, a pinch of salt, and jaggery. Simmer on medium-low heat for 6 to 8 minutes until it reduces to a thick paste and oil separates. Set aside.

  4. 4
    Prepare the Tempering (Talimpu)

    Heat remaining oil in a large wok over medium heat. Add mustard seeds and cumin seeds, letting them splutter.

  5. 5
    Add Crunch and Spices

    Add chana dal, urad dal, raw peanuts, and cashew nuts. Fry until golden brown and crunchy. Toss in broken dry red chilies, slit green chilies, fresh curry leaves, and asafoetida, letting them sizzle.

  6. 6
    Combine Tamarind with Tempering

    Pour thick tamarind paste and 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder directly into the sizzling tempering. Sauté for 2 minutes until combined, then turn off the heat.

  7. 7
    Mix with Rice and Serve

    Add tempering-tamarind mixture in batches to cooled rice. Gently toss until evenly coated. Cover and rest for 30 minutes to 1 hour, then serve at room temperature with fried papad.
