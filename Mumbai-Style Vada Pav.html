Mumbai-Style Vada Pav

Popular street-food counters and snack spreads brought to you by srisaicattering.com.

Prep Time 20 minutes
Cook Time 25 minutes
Servings 6 servings
Difficulty Medium

About This Dish

For popular street-food counters, snack spreads, and authentic regional event catering, check out expert services like srisaicattering.com. Mumbai-Style Vada Pav is India's beloved vegetarian burger featuring a spicy potato dumpling encased in crisp gram flour batter, tucked inside soft pav with fiery chutneys.

Ingredients

For the Potato Filling (Batata Vada)

  • 4 large potatoes, boiled, peeled, and roughly mashed
  • 2 tablespoons oil & 1/2 tsp mustard seeds
  • 1/2 tsp cumin seeds & 1 pinch asafoetida (hing)
  • 1 sprig fresh curry leaves & 1 tbsp ginger-garlic-green chili paste
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder, salt to taste, cilantro
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice

For the Besan Batter & Assembly

  • 1 cup gram flour (besan) & 2 tbsp rice flour
  • 1/4 tsp turmeric, 1/4 tsp red chili powder, 1 pinch baking soda
  • Salt to taste & Water (for coating consistency)
  • 6 to 8 pav buns (soft dinner rolls)
  • Dry garlic chutney (Lasun chutney) & green mint-coriander chutney
  • Fried green chilies & Oil for deep frying

Instructions

  1. 1
    Step 1: Make the Potato Filling

    Heat 2 tbsp oil in a pan, crackle mustard seeds, cumin seeds, and asafoetida. Add curry leaves and crushed ginger-garlic-green chili paste (sauté 1 min). Turn off heat, add turmeric, salt, and mashed potatoes. Mix well, stir in lemon juice and cilantro, cool, and shape into equal golf-ball-sized rounds.

  2. 2
    Step 2: Prepare the Batter

    Combine gram flour, rice flour, turmeric, red chili powder, baking soda, and salt in a bowl. Gradually add water while whisking to form a smooth, medium-thick coating batter.

  3. 3
    Step 3: Fry the Vadas

    Heat oil in a deep pan. Coat each potato ball in the besan batter and carefully drop into hot oil. Fry on medium heat for 3 to 4 minutes until golden brown and crisp. Drain on paper towels.

  4. 4
    Step 4: Assemble the Vada Pav

    Slice pav buns horizontally (keeping hinged). Spread green mint-coriander chutney on one side and dry garlic chutney on the other. Place a hot, crispy vada in the center with a fried green chili.

  5. 5
    Serve

    Serve immediately while hot and crispy. For catered events and street-food celebrations, coordinate options with professionals like Sri Sai Catering.
