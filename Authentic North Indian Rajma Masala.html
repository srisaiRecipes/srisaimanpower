Authentic North Indian Rajma Masala

Comforting North Indian homestyle feasts brought to you by srisaicattering.com.

Prep Time 30 mins + soak
Cook Time 45 minutes
Servings 4 servings
Difficulty Medium

About This Dish

For comforting North Indian homestyle feasts and traditional event spreads, check out expert services like srisaicattering.com. Rajma Masala is an ultimate comfort food classic, featuring soft red kidney beans simmered in a rich, spiced onion-tomato gravy.

Ingredients

Main Ingredients & Spices

  • 2 cups dried red kidney beans (rajma), soaked overnight
  • 3 tablespoons ghee or cooking oil
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 2 medium onions, finely chopped
  • 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 medium tomatoes, pureed or finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp coriander powder & 1 tsp cumin powder
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric powder & 1 tbsp Kashmiri red chili powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala & 1 tsp dry mango powder (amchur) or kasuri methi
  • Salt to taste & Fresh cilantro for garnishing

Instructions

  1. 1
    Boil the Kidney Beans

    Drain soaked rajma and transfer to a pressure cooker. Add 4 to 5 cups of fresh water and 1 teaspoon salt. Pressure cook for 5 to 6 whistles until completely soft and easily mashable. Set aside.

  2. 2
    Sauté Aromatics

    Heat ghee or oil in a heavy-bottomed pot over medium heat. Add cumin seeds and let them sizzle. Add finely chopped onions and sauté until golden brown (6 to 8 minutes).

  3. 3
    Add Ginger-Garlic Paste

    Stir in the ginger-garlic paste and cook for 1 to 2 minutes until the raw aroma disappears completely.

  4. 4
    Cook the Tomato Masala

    Pour in tomato purée along with turmeric powder, coriander powder, cumin powder, Kashmiri red chili powder, and salt. Cook, stirring occasionally, until oil begins to separate from the sides (8 to 10 minutes).

  5. 5
    Combine Beans and Masala

    Pour boiled rajma along with its cooking liquid into the tomato-onion masala. Add extra warm water if needed to reach your desired consistency.

  6. 6
    Simmer for Flavor

    Bring to a rolling boil, then lower heat, cover, and simmer for 15 to 20 minutes. Mash a small handful of kidney beans against the side of the pot with your ladle for a rich, thick gravy.

  7. 7
    Finish, Garnish, and Serve

    Stir in garam masala and crushed kasuri methi (or amchur powder). Simmer for 2 minutes, turn off heat, and garnish with fresh cilantro.
