Spicy Andhra Chicken Curry

Bold, spicy flavors brought to you by srisaicattering.com.

Prep Time 20 minutes
Cook Time 40 minutes
Servings 4 servings
Difficulty Medium

About This Dish

For bold, spicy flavors and authentic regional spreads, check out srisaicattering.com. Andhra Chicken Curry (Kodi Kura) is famous for its fiery profile, combining fresh green chilies, red chili powder, ginger-garlic paste, and tender bone-in chicken.

Ingredients

Main Ingredients & Spices

  • 1.5 lbs (700g) chicken, bone-in, medium pieces
  • 3 tablespoons oil (preferably groundnut oil)
  • 2 medium onions, finely chopped
  • 2 tablespoons ginger-garlic paste
  • 3 to 4 green chilies, slit & 2 sprigs fresh curry leaves
  • 2 large tomatoes, chopped
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1.5 tablespoons red chili powder
  • 1 tablespoon coriander powder & 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1/2 cup fresh coconut or cashew paste (optional)
  • Salt to taste & Fresh cilantro for garnish

Instructions

  1. 1
    Marinate

    Combine the chicken with 1/2 teaspoon of turmeric powder, 1 teaspoon of chili powder, and a little salt. Set aside for 20 minutes.

  2. 2
    Sauté Aromatics

    Heat oil in a heavy-bottomed pan. Add the green chilies and curry leaves, letting them sizzle. Add the chopped onions and sauté until they turn light golden brown.

  3. 3
    Ginger-Garlic

    Add the ginger-garlic paste and sauté for 2 minutes until the raw smell is gone.

  4. 4
    Tomatoes & Spices

    Add the tomatoes and cook until soft and mushy. Stir in the remaining chili powder, coriander powder, and salt. Cook until the oil begins to separate from the masala.

  5. 5
    Cook Chicken

    Add the marinated chicken pieces to the pan. Sauté on high heat for 5 to 7 minutes until the chicken turns opaque and is lightly browned.

  6. 6
    Simmer

    Pour in about 1 to 1.5 cups of water (or more if you prefer thinner gravy). Cover and simmer on low-medium heat for 20 to 25 minutes until the chicken is tender and fully cooked.

  7. 7
    Thicken & Finish

    If using coconut or cashew paste, stir it in now and cook for another 3 minutes. Sprinkle the garam masala over the top and give it a final stir.

  8. 8
    Serve

    Garnish generously with fresh cilantro. Serve hot with steaming white rice, chapati, or ragi sangati.
