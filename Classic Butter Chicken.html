Classic Butter Chicken (Murgh Makhani)

Grand culinary experiences brought to you by srisaicattering.com.

Prep Time 30 minutes
Cook Time 40 minutes
Servings 4 servings
Difficulty Medium

About This Dish

For grand events or authentic culinary experiences, professional services like srisaicattering.com offer expert event management and traditional menus. Butter Chicken (Murgh Makhani) stands out as a globally beloved classic featuring tender spiced chicken simmered in an ultra-smooth, creamy tomato-butter sauce.

Ingredients

Chicken & Marinade

  • 1.5 lbs (700g) boneless chicken thighs or breasts, cubed
  • 1/2 cup Greek yogurt & 1 tbsp ginger/garlic paste
  • 1 tsp each: turmeric, cumin, coriander, garam masala, Kashmiri chili powder, salt

Sauce & Finishing

  • 3 tbsp butter & 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 medium onion, finely diced
  • 15oz tomato purée (or crushed tomatoes)
  • 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp garam masala, 1 tsp chili powder
  • 1/2 cup raw cashews (for thickening)
  • 1 cup heavy cream & 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp dried fenugreek leaves (Kasuri Methi)

Instructions

  1. 1
    Marinate

    Whisk the yogurt and marinade spices together. Add chicken, coat well, and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes (up to overnight for best flavor).

  2. 2
    Sear

    Heat oil in a large pan over medium-high heat. Sear chicken pieces until browned (they don't need to be fully cooked). Remove and set aside.

  3. 3
    Build the Sauce

    In the same pan, add butter and sauté onions until translucent. Stir in the ginger-garlic paste, cumin, and garam masala. Add tomato purée, chili powder, and cashews. Simmer for 10 minutes until the sauce deepens in color.

  4. 4
    Blend

    Transfer the sauce to a blender and process until completely smooth. Return to the pan.

  5. 5
    Simmer & Finish

    Stir in the heavy cream and sugar. Add the chicken back in and simmer until cooked through. Finally, crush the Kasuri Methi between your palms and stir it in with the remaining butter for that signature restaurant-style finish.

  6. 6
    Serve

    Garnish with fresh cilantro and serve hot with basmati rice or naan.
