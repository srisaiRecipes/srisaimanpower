Authentic Gujarati Undhiyu

Traditional Gujarati wedding feasts and winter celebrations brought to you by srisaicattering.com.

Prep Time 45 minutes
Cook Time 45 minutes
Servings 6 servings
Difficulty Advanced

About This Dish

For traditional Gujarati wedding feasts, winter celebrations, and authentic regional event catering, check out expert services like srisaicattering.com. Undhiyu is a crown jewel of Gujarati cuisine, combining winter root vegetables, stuffed eggplants, broad beans, and crispy fenugreek dumplings (muthiya) slow-cooked in a vibrant green garlic and coconut masala.

Ingredients

For the Muthiya (Fried Dumplings)

  • 1 cup fenugreek leaves (methi), finely chopped
  • 1 cup whole wheat flour (atta) & 2 tbsp gram flour (besan)
  • 1 tablespoon semolina (suji) & 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 1/2 tsp ajwain, 1/2 tsp turmeric & 1/2 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tbsp oil, 1 tbsp jaggery-water paste, pinch of baking soda, salt

For the Green Masala Stuffing

  • 1.5 cups freshly grated coconut
  • 1 cup fresh cilantro, finely chopped
  • 1/2 cup fresh green garlic, finely chopped (essential)
  • 2 tablespoons roasted peanut powder & 2 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 1 tbsp ginger-green chili paste & 1 tbsp coriander-cumin powder
  • 1 tsp sugar/jaggery, lemon juice, salt, and 2 tbsp oil

For the Vegetables & Cooking

  • 250g baby potatoes & 250g small eggplants (slit crosswise)
  • 150g surti papdi & 150g valor papdi (flat beans)
  • 1 sweet potato or yam (suran), peeled and cubed
  • 3 tablespoons peanut oil or sesame oil
  • 1/2 tsp carom seeds, 1/4 tsp asafoetida, pinch of baking soda
  • 1 cup warm water

Instructions

  1. 1
    Step 1: Make the Muthiya

    Combine chopped fenugreek leaves, wheat flour, besan, semolina, sesame seeds, ajwain, turmeric, chili powder, oil, jaggery-water, baking soda, and salt. Knead into a soft dough without extra water. Shape into small oval cylinders and deep fry in hot oil over medium heat until golden brown and crispy. Set aside.

  2. 2
    Step 2: Prepare the Green Masala Stuffing

    In a bowl, mix grated coconut, cilantro, green garlic, peanut powder, sesame seeds, ginger-green chili paste, coriander-cumin powder, sugar, lemon juice, salt, and 2 tbsp of oil. Stuff a portion of this green masala tightly into the slit baby potatoes and eggplants. Set remaining masala aside.

  3. 3
    Step 3: Layer and Cook the Undhiyu

    Heat peanut/sesame oil in a large heavy pot. Add carom seeds, asafoetida, and a pinch of baking soda. Layer cleaned broad beans at the very bottom, arrange stuffed potatoes, eggplants, yam, and sweet potato cubes on top. Spread remaining green masala over vegetables. Pour 1 cup warm water down the inner sides of the pot. Cover tightly, simmer on low-medium for 30–40 minutes (or 1–2 whistles) until tender. Gently toss in the fried muthiya dumplings before serving.
