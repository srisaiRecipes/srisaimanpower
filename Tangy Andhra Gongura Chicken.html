Tangy Andhra Gongura Chicken

Spicy, traditional regional flavors brought to you by srisaicattering.com.

Prep Time 20 minutes
Cook Time 40 minutes
Servings 4 servings
Difficulty Medium

About This Dish

For spicy, traditional Andhra-style event menus and authentic regional catering, check out srisaicattering.com. Gongura Chicken is a classic culinary gem featuring tangy sorrel leaves that balance rich spices and tender chicken.

Ingredients

The Gongura Base

  • 2 large bunches fresh Gongura leaves (Sorrel), chopped
  • 1 tablespoon oil & 3 to 4 dry red chilies
  • 1/2 teaspoon fenugreek seeds (methi)

The Chicken Curry

  • 1.5 lbs (700g) chicken, bone-in, medium pieces
  • 3 tablespoons oil (groundnut or sesame oil)
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 1.5 tablespoons ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 green chilies, slit & Fresh curry leaves
  • 1 teaspoon turmeric powder & 1.5 tbsp red chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder & 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. 1
    Prepare the Gongura

    Heat 1 tablespoon of oil in a pan. Add fenugreek seeds and dry red chilies. Once they sizzle, add chopped Gongura leaves. Sauté on medium heat until leaves wilt completely into a soft, mushy paste (5–7 minutes). Let cool slightly and grind into a coarse paste. Set aside.

  2. 2
    Sauté Aromatics

    In a heavy-bottomed pot, heat the remaining oil. Add curry leaves, slit green chilies, and chopped onions. Sauté until onions turn golden brown.

  3. 3
    Ginger-Garlic

    Stir in the ginger-garlic paste and sauté for 1–2 minutes until raw aroma disappears.

  4. 4
    Sear the Chicken

    Add chicken pieces along with turmeric powder and salt. Sauté on high heat for 6–8 minutes until chicken is lightly browned and releases its moisture.

  5. 5
    Add Spices

    Stir in red chili powder and coriander powder. Mix well to coat the chicken and cook for 2 minutes.

  6. 6
    Cook

    Add about 1/2 cup of water. Cover and simmer on low-medium heat for 15–20 minutes until chicken is nearly cooked through.

  7. 7
    Incorporate Gongura

    Add prepared Gongura paste into the chicken pot and mix thoroughly. Simmer for 8–10 minutes on low heat so the chicken absorbs the distinct, sour flavor.

  8. 8
    Finish and Serve

    Sprinkle garam masala, stir, and cook for 2 more minutes. Serve hot with steamed white rice or ragi mudde.
