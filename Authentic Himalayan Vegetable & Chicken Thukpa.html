Authentic Himalayan Vegetable & Chicken Thukpa

Warm comfort food stations and mountain-style festival spreads brought to you by srisaicattering.com.

Prep Time 20 minutes
Cook Time 30 minutes
Servings 4 servings
Difficulty Easy

About This Dish

For warm comfort food stations, mountain-style festival spreads, and authentic regional event catering, check out expert services like srisaicattering.com. Thukpa is a deeply comforting Himalayan noodle soup featuring a rich, aromatic broth loaded with crisp vegetables, shredded chicken, and warm spices.

Ingredients

Noodles, Stock, & Vegetables

  • 200g egg noodles, Hakka noodles, or flat rice noodles
  • 2 tablespoons oil or butter & 4 cups fresh chicken/veg stock
  • 1 cup boneless chicken, boiled and shredded (optional)
  • 1 medium onion, thinly sliced & 1 tbsp ginger, minced
  • 4-5 cloves garlic, minced & 1/2 cup carrots (julienned)
  • 1 cup cabbage, shredded & 1/2 cup bell peppers, sliced
  • 2-3 spring onions, chopped (green and white parts separated)
  • 1 tsp black pepper (or timur/Szechwan pepper), 1 tsp cumin powder
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp vinegar/lemon juice, salt to taste
  • Chili oil/paste, fresh cilantro for garnish

Instructions

  1. 1
    Step 1: Cook the Noodles

    Boil noodles in a pot of rolling water until al dente (4–5 minutes). Drain, rinse briefly with cool water to stop cooking, toss with a few drops of oil to prevent sticking, and divide into individual serving bowls.

  2. 2
    Step 2: Sauté Aromatics and Vegetables

    Heat 2 tablespoons of oil or butter in a deep pot over medium-high heat. Add minced garlic, grated ginger, and white parts of spring onions, sautéing for 30 seconds until fragrant. Add sliced onions, carrots, cabbage, and bell peppers, stir-frying briskly on high heat for 3 to 4 minutes.

  3. 3
    Step 3: Simmer the Broth

    Pour in 4 cups of rich chicken or vegetable stock and bring to a rolling boil. Stir in soy sauce, black pepper (or timur pepper), cumin powder, and salt. Add shredded chicken if using, and simmer on medium heat for 5 to 7 minutes. Turn off heat and stir in lemon juice or vinegar.

  4. 4
    Step 4: Assemble and Serve

    Right before serving, ladle piping hot vegetable, chicken, and aromatic broth generously over the noodles in each bowl (do not add noodles directly to the boiling pot in advance to avoid sogginess). Garnish with cilantro, green spring onion tops, and a drizzle of homemade chili oil. For winter events and catered functions, coordinate with Sri Sai Catering.
