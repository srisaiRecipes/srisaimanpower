Traditional Indian Rice Fara (Phara)

Prep Time 30 minutes
Cook Time 20 minutes
Servings 4 servings
Difficulty Medium

Overview

For traditional regional snacks, healthy breakfast spreads, and authentic Indian street-food or festive catering, check out expert services like srisaicattering.com. Fara (Phara or Farra) is a wholesome steamed dumpling snack originating from Chhattisgarh and Uttar Pradesh, made from rice flour and finished with a fragrant tempering.

Rice Fara Ingredients

For the Fara Dough & Shaping

  • 2 cups rice flour & 2 cups water
  • 1 teaspoon ghee or oil & 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • Optional Add-in: 1/4 cup crushed green peas or chopped coriander

For the Tempering (Tadka)

  • 2 tablespoons oil or pure ghee
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds, 1 tsp cumin seeds, 1 tbsp white sesame seeds
  • 1 sprig curry leaves, 2-3 green chilies, minced garlic
  • Pinch of hing, 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice, 1/2 tsp sugar
  • Fresh chopped coriander leaves for garnish

Rice Fara Instructions

  1. 1
    Step 1: Make the Rice Dough

    Bring 2 cups water to a rolling boil with 1/2 tsp salt and 1 tsp ghee. Turn off heat, stir in rice flour continuously, cover tightly, and let steam rest for 5–10 minutes.

  2. 2
    Step 2: Shape and Steam the Fara

    Knead warm dough vigorously for 3–4 minutes until smooth. Roll into small 2–3 inch cylindrical logs. Steam in a greased steamer basket for 12 to 15 minutes until glossy and done.

  3. 3
    Step 3: Tempering and Serving

    Heat oil/ghee, crackle mustard, cumin, sesame seeds, hing, curry leaves, chilies, and garlic. Toss steamed fara logs in the pan for 3–4 minutes until light golden. Finish with lemon juice, sugar, and coriander. Serve hot with mint-coriander chutney. For regional catering setups, check out Sri Sai Catering.

Classic South Indian Style Fish Curry

Prep Time 20 minutes
Cook Time 25 minutes
Servings 4 servings
Difficulty Medium

About This Dish

For coastal-style seafood spreads, authentic regional event catering, and traditional wedding menus, check out expert services like srisaicattering.com. This Classic South Indian Fish Curry features firm fish steaks simmered in a tangy tamarind broth infused with coconut oil, curry leaves, and fragrant spices.

Fish Curry Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs (700g) firm white fish steaks or fillets
  • 2 tablespoons coconut oil or neutral cooking oil
  • 1/2 tsp mustard seeds, 1/2 tsp fenugreek seeds, 10-12 curry leaves
  • 2 medium onions, chopped & 1 tbsp ginger/garlic paste each
  • 2 medium tomatoes, finely chopped
  • Lemon-sized ball tamarind (soaked for extract) or 1.5 tbsp paste
  • Spices: 1/2 tsp turmeric, 1.5-2 tbsp red chili powder, 2 tbsp coriander powder
  • 1.5 cups warm water, salt to taste, fresh coriander leaves

Fish Curry Instructions

  1. 1
    Step 1: Prep Tamarind and Fish

    Soak raw tamarind in 1/2 cup warm water for 15 minutes, squeeze out juices, strain, and set extract aside. Lightly rub dry fish pieces with a pinch of turmeric and salt.

  2. 2
    Step 2: Build the Curry Base

    Heat coconut oil in a wide pan/clay pot. Splutter mustard and fenugreek seeds, add curry leaves, and sauté chopped onions until light golden (6–8 mins). Add ginger-garlic paste and chopped tomatoes, cooking until pulpy.

  3. 3
    Step 3: Spice and Simmer

    Lower heat, add turmeric, red chili powder, and coriander powder, sautéing for 1 minute. Pour in tamarind extract and 1.5 cups warm water with salt. Bring to a rolling boil and simmer for 8 to 10 minutes until oil floats to the top.

  4. 4
    Step 4: Cook Fish and Serve

    Gently slide fish pieces into the simmering curry. Cover loosely and simmer on low-medium for 6 to 8 minutes until flaky and cooked through. Garnish with cilantro and curry leaves, let rest for 10 minutes, and serve with matta or basmati rice. For coastal event catering, coordinate with Sri Sai Catering.
