Authentic Kashmiri Dum Aloo

Traditional North Indian vegetarian spreads and festive celebrations brought to you by srisaicattering.com.

Prep Time 20 minutes
Cook Time 45 minutes
Servings 4 servings
Difficulty Medium

About This Dish

For traditional North Indian vegetarian spreads, festive celebrations, and authentic regional catering, check out expert services like srisaicattering.com. Kashmiri Dum Aloo is a beloved classic featuring tender baby potatoes slow-cooked (dum) in a fragrant, spiced yogurt gravy without onions or garlic.

Ingredients

Potatoes & Yogurt-Spice Gravy

  • 1.5 lbs (700g) small baby potatoes (uniform in size)
  • Oil or Ghee (for deep frying) & fork/toothpick for piercing
  • 1.5 cups plain yogurt (curd), beaten smooth
  • 2 tablespoons Kashmiri red chili powder
  • 1.5 tablespoons dry ginger powder (sonth)
  • 1.5 tablespoons fennel powder (saunf)
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric & 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 1/2 teaspoon asafoetida (hing) dissolved in 2 tbsp water
  • 3 tablespoons mustard oil or ghee
  • Whole spices: 1 black cardamom, 3 green cardamoms, 1-inch cinnamon, 3-4 cloves, 1 bay leaf, 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 cup water or vegetable stock, Salt to taste, Fresh cilantro

Instructions

  1. 1
    Boil and Peel

    Boil baby potatoes in salted water until 70% cooked (firm-tender, do not overcook). Peel while warm and prick all over using a fork or toothpick so gravy can penetrate inside.

  2. 2
    Deep Fry

    Heat oil or ghee in a heavy pan over medium-high heat. Fry pricked potatoes until outer skins turn golden brown and crisp. Remove with a slotted spoon and set aside.

  3. 3
    Mix the Yogurt Spice Base

    In a large bowl, whisk smooth yogurt with Kashmiri red chili powder, dry ginger powder, fennel powder, turmeric, and a pinch of salt. Set aside.

  4. 4
    Temper Spices

    Heat 3 tablespoons mustard oil (or ghee) in a heavy-bottomed pot over medium heat until smoking hot, then lower heat. Add asafoetida water (watch for splatters), whole spices, and cumin seeds, letting them sizzle.

  5. 5
    Incorporate Yogurt (The Crucial Step)

    Lower heat to minimum (or turn off flame temporarily to prevent curdling). Pour spiced yogurt mixture directly into the pot, stirring continuously until incorporated and oil separates from edges.

  6. 6
    Simmer the Potatoes

    Add fried baby potatoes into the simmering yogurt gravy, pour in 1 cup of water, and mix gently.

  7. 7
    Dum (Slow Cook), Finish, and Serve

    Cover pot tightly or seal with foil. Simmer on low heat for 15 to 20 minutes so potatoes absorb spices and soften completely. Sprinkle garam masala, garnish with cilantro, and serve hot with naan, parotta, or Basmati rice. For large gatherings, coordinate with Sri Sai Catering.
