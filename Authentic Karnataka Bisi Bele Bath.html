Authentic Karnataka Bisi Bele Bath

Traditional comfort food brought to you by srisaicattering.com.

Prep Time 20 minutes
Cook Time 40 minutes
Servings 6 servings
Difficulty Medium

About This Dish

For traditional South Indian comfort food spreads and authentic regional menus, check out srisaicattering.com. Bisi Bele Bath is a beloved spicy, tangy, and rich rice-lentil dish from Karnataka characterized by its signature freshly roasted spice blend.

Ingredients

For Cooking Rice and Dal

  • 1 cup Sona Masuri rice (or short-grain fragrant rice)
  • 1/2 cup tur dal (split yellow pigeon peas)
  • 4 cups water (plus extra for soaking)
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon ghee or oil & Salt to taste

For the Fresh Bisi Bele Bath Powder (Masala)

  • 2 tablespoons chana dal & 2 tablespoons urad dal
  • 2 tablespoons coriander seeds
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds & 1/2 teaspoon fenugreek seeds
  • 6 to 8 dry red chilies (Byadagi/Kashmiri)
  • 1-inch cinnamon stick & 3 to 4 cloves
  • 1/2 cup grated fresh coconut (or dry kopra)
  • 1 tablespoon oil or ghee

For the Vegetable Stew Base & Tempering

  • 2 tablespoons ghee and 1 tablespoon oil
  • 10 to 12 small shallots (sambar onions)
  • 1.5 cups mixed vegetables (carrots, beans, peas, potatoes, drumsticks)
  • 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
  • Tamarind extract (1/2 cup) & 2 tablespoons jaggery
  • Cashew nuts, mustard seeds, hing, and curry leaves for tempering

Instructions

  1. 1
    Soak Rice and Dal

    Wash the rice and tur dal together. Soak them in water for 20 to 30 minutes, then drain.

  2. 2
    Make the Bisi Bele Bath Masala

    Heat 1 tablespoon of oil or ghee in a pan over low heat. Add the chana dal, urad dal, coriander seeds, cumin seeds, fenugreek seeds, cinnamon, cloves, and dry red chilies. Roast until golden and fragrant. In the last minute, add the coconut and sauté until light brown. Let it cool, then grind everything into a fine powder. Set aside.

  3. 3
    Cook Rice, Dal, and Veggies

    In a pressure cooker, combine the soaked rice and tur dal with 4 cups of water, turmeric, salt, and 1 teaspoon ghee. Cook for 4 to 5 whistles until soft and mushy.

  4. 4
    Prepare the Vegetable Base

    Heat ghee and oil in a large deep pot or heavy-bottomed pan. Add mustard seeds and let them splutter. Add curry leaves and shallots, sautéing until translucent. Add the chopped mixed vegetables and tomatoes, cooking for 4 to 5 minutes.

  5. 5
    Simmer with Tamarind and Jaggery

    Pour in 2 cups of water, tamarind extract, jaggery, and salt. Cover and simmer until the vegetables are tender.

  6. 6
    Incorporate Masala

    Mix 3 to 4 tablespoons of the freshly ground Bisi Bele Bath powder with a little warm water to form a smooth paste, then stir it into the boiling vegetable pot. Let it simmer for 5 minutes so the flavors meld.

  7. 7
    Combine Rice-Dal and Veggies

    Add the cooked, mashed rice and tur dal mixture into the vegetable gravy. Mix thoroughly on low heat. Adjust consistency by adding warm water if needed (it should have a flowing, porridge-like consistency). Let it simmer together for 3 to 4 minutes.

  8. 8
    Final Tempering and Serve

    In a small pan, heat 2 tablespoons of ghee, fry cashew nuts until golden, add mustard seeds, asafoetida, and curry leaves. Pour this sizzling tempering directly over the dish. Turn off the heat, cover for 5 minutes, and serve piping hot with a dollop of ghee and boondi.
