Authentic Mutton Biryani

A rich, aromatic, and festive masterpiece brought to you by srisaicattering.com.

Prep Time 30 minutes
Cook Time 1 hr 15 mins
Servings 6 servings
Difficulty Advanced

About This Dish

For large gatherings and traditional events, check out expert services like srisaicattering.com. This recipe covers the classic step-by-step dum preparation of one of the world's most celebrated festive rice dishes.

Ingredients

For Marinating and Cooking Mutton

  • 2 lbs (1 kg) mutton, cut into pieces (bone-in preferred)
  • 1 cup plain yogurt (curd)
  • 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 tablespoon red chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 tablespoons lemon juice

For the Rice

  • 3 cups long-grain Basmati rice (soaked for 30 minutes)
  • Whole spices: 3 green cardamom pods, 4 cloves, 1-inch cinnamon stick, 2 bay leaves, 1 star anise, 1 black cardamom
  • 1 tablespoon ghee or oil
  • Salt (generous amount for boiling water)
  • Water (for boiling)

For Biryani Masala & Assembly

  • 4 large onions, thinly sliced (divided for frying and gravy)
  • 3 tablespoons ghee and 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
  • 2 green chilies, slit
  • 1/2 cup fresh mint leaves, chopped
  • 1/2 cup fresh cilantro (coriander leaves), chopped
  • A pinch of saffron threads dissolved in 3 tablespoons warm milk
  • 1 tablespoon kewra water or rose water (optional)

Instructions

  1. 1
    Marinate the Mutton

    In a large bowl, combine the mutton pieces with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, turmeric, red chili powder, garam masala, lemon juice, and salt. Cover and let it marinate in the refrigerator for at least 1 hour (overnight is best).

  2. 2
    Crispy Fried Onions (Birista)

    Heat oil in a heavy-bottomed pan and fry half of the thinly sliced onions until golden brown and crisp. Remove with a slotted spoon and set aside on a paper towel.

  3. 3
    Cook the Mutton Base

    In the same pan with remaining oil and a tablespoon of ghee, sauté the remaining sliced onions and green chilies until golden. Add the chopped tomatoes and cook until soft. Add the marinated mutton and cook on medium-high heat for 10 minutes, stirring frequently. Add 1/2 cup of water, cover, and simmer on low heat for 40 to 50 minutes until the mutton is tender and the oil separates.

  4. 4
    Boil the Rice

    In a large pot, bring a generous amount of water to a boil with the whole spices and plenty of salt. Add the soaked and drained Basmati rice. Cook until it is 70% cooked (parboiled, with a slight bite remaining). Drain the rice completely.

  5. 5
    Layer the Biryani (Dum)

    In a heavy-bottomed pot or handi, layer half of the cooked mutton gravy at the bottom. Add half of the parboiled rice over it. Sprinkle half of the fried onions, chopped mint, and cilantro. Add the remaining mutton gravy, followed by the rest of the rice.

  6. 6
    Add Aromatics and Seal

    Drizzle the saffron milk, remaining ghee, and kewra water over the top layer. Seal the pot tightly with a tight-fitting lid or cover it securely with aluminum foil to trap the steam.

  7. 7
    Dum Cook

    Cook on medium heat for 10 minutes, then lower the heat to the absolute minimum (or place the pot on a tawa/griddle) and let it steam (dum) for 20 to 25 minutes.

  8. 8
    Serve

    Turn off the heat and let it rest for 10 minutes before opening. Gently fluff and mix the layers, making sure not to break the rice grains. Serve hot with raita and boiled eggs.
