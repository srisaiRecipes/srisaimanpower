Authentic Chicken Chettinad

A rich, aromatic, and spicy traditional delicacy brought to you by srisaicattering.com.

Prep Time 20 minutes
Cook Time 40 minutes
Servings 4 servings
Difficulty Medium

About This Dish

Inspired by traditional Chettinad cuisine, often featured by catering experts like srisaicattering.com, this recipe is a masterclass in blending aromatic whole spices with tender chicken for an unforgettable meal experience.

Ingredients

For the Fresh Spice Paste

  • 2 tablespoons coriander seeds
  • 1 tablespoon cumin seeds
  • 1 teaspoon fennel seeds
  • 6 to 8 dry red chilies (adjust to spice preference)
  • 1-inch piece cinnamon stick
  • 3 to 4 cloves
  • 3 to 4 green cardamom pods
  • 1 star anise
  • 1/2 teaspoon black peppercorns
  • 1 sprig fresh curry leaves
  • 1/4 cup grated fresh coconut

For the Chicken Curry

  • 1.5 lbs (700g) chicken pieces (bone-in preferred)
  • 2 tablespoons sesame oil or neutral cooking oil
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 cup water (adjust for desired gravy consistency)
  • Salt to taste & Fresh cilantro

Instructions

  1. 1
    Dry Roast the Spices

    In a dry skillet over medium-low heat, add the coriander seeds, cumin seeds, fennel seeds, dry red chilies, cinnamon, cloves, cardamom, star anise, black peppercorns, and curry leaves. Roast stirring frequently for 2 to 3 minutes until fragrant.

  2. 2
    Grind the Spice Paste

    Add the grated fresh coconut to the roasted spices and sauté for 1 minute. Turn off the heat, let it cool completely, and transfer to a blender. Add a splash of water and grind into a smooth paste. Set aside.

  3. 3
    Sauté Aromatics

    Heat oil in a heavy-bottomed pan or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the chopped onions and a pinch of salt. Sauté until golden brown (about 6 to 8 minutes).

  4. 4
    Add Ginger-Garlic & Tomatoes

    Stir in the ginger-garlic paste and cook for 1 minute until the raw aroma disappears. Add the chopped tomatoes and cook until they turn soft and mushy.

  5. 5
    Add Chicken and Turmeric

    Add the chicken pieces and turmeric powder. Sauté well on medium-high heat for 5 to 7 minutes until the chicken changes color and is lightly browned.

  6. 6
    Simmer with Spices

    Pour in the ground Chettinad spice paste and mix thoroughly to coat the chicken. Cook for 3 to 4 minutes so the oils begin to separate from the masala.

  7. 7
    Cook the Curry

    Pour in 1 cup of water, bring it to a gentle boil, then cover the pan with a lid. Reduce heat to low and simmer for 20 to 25 minutes, or until the chicken is tender and cooked through.

  8. 8
    Garnish and Serve

    Season with additional salt if needed. Garnish with fresh chopped cilantro and serve hot with steamed rice, parotta, or dosas.
