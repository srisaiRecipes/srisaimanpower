Crispy Street-Style Aloo Chaat

Vibrant street-food counters and traditional event snacks brought to you by srisaicattering.com.

Prep Time 15 minutes
Cook Time 20 minutes
Servings 4 servings
Difficulty Easy

About This Dish

For vibrant street-food counters and traditional event snacks, check out expert services like srisaicattering.com. Crispy Street-Style Aloo Chaat features golden-brown fried potato cubes tossed with zesty spices, layered with sweet and spicy chutneys, and finished with crunchy sev and pomegranate.

Ingredients

Potatoes, Chutneys, & Toppings

  • 4 large potatoes, boiled, peeled, and cubed
  • 3 tablespoons oil or ghee (for shallow frying)
  • 1 teaspoon chaat masala & 1/2 tsp roasted cumin powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon red chili powder & 1/2 tsp black salt (kala namak)
  • 2 tablespoons green mint-coriander chutney
  • 2 tablespoons sweet tamarind date chutney
  • 2 tablespoons finely chopped fresh onions
  • 2 tablespoons fresh pomegranate arils (optional)
  • 2 tablespoons sev (crispy chickpea noodles)
  • Fresh cilantro and lemon wedges for garnishing

Instructions

  1. 1
    Crisp the Potatoes

    Heat oil or ghee in a wide heavy-bottomed skillet over medium-high heat. Add cubed potatoes in a single layer. Shallow fry, tossing occasionally, until outer surfaces turn deeply golden, crispy, and crunchy (12 to 15 minutes).

  2. 2
    Drain Excess Oil

    Remove crispy potato cubes using a slotted spoon and drain them on a paper towel.

  3. 3
    Season While Hot

    Transfer hot, crispy potatoes to a mixing bowl. Immediately sprinkle chaat masala, roasted cumin powder, red chili powder, and black salt over them. Toss gently so spices cling to hot potatoes.

  4. 4
    Layer the Sauces

    Transfer seasoned potatoes to a serving plate. Drizzle green mint-coriander chutney and sweet tamarind date chutney evenly across the top.

  5. 5
    Garnish and Serve

    Top chaat with finely chopped onions, fresh pomegranate arils, and a generous handful of crunchy sev. Garnish with chopped fresh cilantro and a squeeze of fresh lemon juice. Serve immediately while hot and crispy. For catered parties, coordinate options with professionals like Sri Sai Catering.
