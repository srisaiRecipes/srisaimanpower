Traditional Gulab Jamun

Festive dessert spreads, wedding banquets, and traditional Indian sweet catering brought to you by srisaicattering.com.

Prep Time 30 minutes
Cook Time 30 minutes
Servings 20 to 25 pieces
Difficulty Medium

About This Dish

For festive dessert spreads, wedding banquets, and traditional Indian sweet catering, check out expert services like srisaicattering.com. Gulab Jamun is an undisputed favorite of Indian celebrations, featuring spongy milk-solid spheres soaked deep in a warm, fragrant rose-cardamom sugar syrup.

Ingredients

For the Sugar Syrup (Chashni)

  • 2.5 cups granulated sugar & 2.5 cups water
  • 4 to 5 green cardamoms, lightly crushed
  • 1 teaspoon rose water or kewra water
  • A pinch of saffron strands (optional)
  • 1/2 teaspoon lemon juice (prevents sugar crystallization)

For the Gulab Jamun Dough & Frying

  • 1 cup milk powder (full-fat milk powder works best)
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour (maida) & 2 tablespoons ghee (melted)
  • 1/4 cup warm milk (plus a few extra drops if needed)
  • 1/4 teaspoon baking powder & a pinch of cardamom powder
  • Ghee or neutral oil for deep frying (low-temperature frying)

Instructions

  1. 1
    Step 1: Make the Sugar Syrup

    In a wide saucepan, combine sugar, water, and crushed cardamoms. Bring to a boil until sugar dissolves. Add lemon juice and saffron strands, simmering for 6 to 8 minutes until it achieves a light honey consistency (sticky, not multi-thread). Stir in rose/kewra water, turn off heat, and keep warm.

  2. 2
    Step 2: Prepare the Dough

    Combine milk powder, maida, baking powder, and cardamom powder in a bowl. Pour in melted ghee and mix with fingertips until resembling fine breadcrumbs. Gradually pour in warm milk a tablespoon at a time, gently bringing it together into a soft, sticky dough without vigorous kneading. Rest for 5 minutes.

  3. 3
    Step 3: Shape the Balls

    Grease palms with a few drops of ghee/oil. Pinch small marble-sized portions of dough and roll smoothly between palms into crack-free balls (they expand while frying). Keep covered with a damp cloth.

  4. 4
    Step 4: Fry on Low Heat

    Heat oil/ghee in a heavy kadai over very low heat. Slip balls into the warm oil in batches, swirling gently for 8 to 10 minutes until they turn a rich, uniform golden-brown. Drain excess oil on paper towels.

  5. 5
    Step 5: Soak and Serve

    Transfer warm fried jamuns directly into warm sugar syrup. Let soak undisturbed for 1 to 2 hours. Serve warm or chilled, garnished with pistachios or almonds. For grand celebrations and sweet catering menus, coordinate with Sri Sai Catering.
