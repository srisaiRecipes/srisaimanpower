Mumbai-Style Pav Bhaji

Vibrant street-food counters and party snacks brought to you by srisaicattering.com.

Prep Time 20 minutes
Cook Time 30 minutes
Servings 4 servings
Difficulty Easy

About This Dish

For vibrant street-food counters, party snacks, and authentic regional event catering, check out expert services like srisaicattering.com. Mumbai-Style Pav Bhaji is an iconic street food favorite featuring a spicy, buttery vegetable mash served alongside soft, butter-toasted dinner rolls.

Ingredients

For the Bhaji (Vegetable Mash)

  • 4 medium potatoes, peeled and chopped
  • 1 cup cauliflower florets & 1/2 cup green peas
  • 1/2 cup carrots, chopped & 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 3 tablespoons butter (plus extra for topping) & 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 large capsicum (green bell pepper), finely chopped
  • 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste & 3 medium tomatoes
  • 2.5 tablespoons Pav Bhaji masala powder
  • 1 tbsp Kashmiri red chili powder & 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
  • Salt to taste, 1.5 cups water, fresh cilantro & lemon juice

For the Pav (Bread)

  • 8 to 10 pav buns (soft dinner rolls)
  • 3 to 4 tablespoons butter (for toasting the pav)
  • A pinch of Pav Bhaji masala and chopped cilantro

Instructions

  1. 1
    Step 1: Boil and Mash the Vegetables

    Pressure cook chopped potatoes, cauliflower florets, green peas, carrots, and 1.5 cups of water for 4 to 5 whistles until completely soft. Without draining water, use a potato masher to mash vegetables directly in the pot until smooth. Set aside.

  2. 2
    Step 2: Prepare the Bhaji Masala Base

    Heat 1 tbsp oil and 2 tbsp butter in a large pan over medium heat. Sauté onions until translucent, add capsicum and ginger-garlic paste (cook 2–3 mins), then add tomatoes, turmeric, Kashmiri chili powder, and salt. Cook until tomatoes are mushy and butter separates.

  3. 3
    Simmer and Finish Bhaji

    Pour mashed veggies into the pan with Pav Bhaji masala powder. Add a warm water splash if needed, simmer on low for 10–12 minutes, stirring and mashing frequently. Finish with a tablespoon of butter, lemon juice, and cilantro.

  4. 4
    Step 3: Toast the Pav

    Heat a flat tawa, melt a tablespoon of butter, and sprinkle a tiny pinch of Pav Bhaji masala and cilantro. Slice pav buns horizontally (keeping hinged) and press onto the bubbly butter mixture to toast crisp.

  5. 5
    Step 4: Serve

    Serve piping hot bhaji topped with a generous pat of butter, alongside buttery toasted pav, chopped raw onions, and lemon wedges. For catered events, coordinate options with professionals like Sri Sai Catering.
