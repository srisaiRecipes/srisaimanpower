Restaurant-Style Dal Makhani

Luxurious North Indian feast spreads brought to you by srisaicattering.com.

Prep Time 30 mins + soak
Cook Time 1 hr 30 mins
Servings 6 servings
Difficulty Medium-Advanced

About This Dish

For luxurious North Indian feast spreads and authentic celebratory menus, check out expert services like srisaicattering.com. Dal Makhani is an iconic culinary masterpiece defined by whole black lentils and kidney beans slow-cooked to creamy perfection with butter and heavy cream.

Ingredients

Lentils, Butter, & Spices

  • 1 cup whole black lentils (sabut urad dal)
  • 1/4 cup red kidney beans (rajma)
  • 3 cups water (plus extra for soaking/boiling)
  • 3 tablespoons pure butter (white butter or unsalted)
  • 2 tablespoons ghee or cooking oil
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds & 1 large onion, finely grated
  • 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste & 1 cup tomato purée
  • 1 tbsp Kashmiri red chili powder & 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 1/2 cup heavy whipping cream (malai)
  • 1 tablespoon dried fenugreek leaves (kasuri methi) & Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. 1
    Soak the Lentils

    Wash whole black urad dal and red kidney beans together thoroughly. Soak in plenty of water overnight (at least 8 hours). Drain before cooking.

  2. 2
    Boil Dal and Rajma

    Transfer soaked lentils and beans to a pressure cooker with 4 cups fresh water, a pinch of salt, and 1 tablespoon butter. Pressure cook for 6 to 8 whistles on medium-low heat until exceptionally soft and mushy. Mash lightly with a ladle.

  3. 3
    Sauté Aromatics

    In a separate large, heavy-bottomed pan, heat ghee and 1 tablespoon of butter over medium heat. Add cumin seeds and let them crackle, then add grated onion and sauté until golden brown.

  4. 4
    Add Ginger-Garlic & Tomatoes

    Stir in ginger-garlic paste and cook for 1 minute. Pour in tomato purée and Kashmiri red chili powder. Cook, stirring frequently, until oil or butter separates from the edges.

  5. 5
    Combine Lentils

    Pour cooked and mashed urad dal-rajma mixture into the tomato-onion masala and stir thoroughly. Add a little warm water if the mixture is too thick.

  6. 6
    Slow Simmer (The Secret Step)

    Bring dal to a gentle boil, then lower heat to a complete minimum. Simmer uncovered, stirring occasionally, for 30 to 45 minutes for a creamy, restaurant-style texture.

  7. 7
    Add Cream, Butter, and Serve

    Stir in heavy whipping cream, another generous tablespoon of butter, crushed kasuri methi, and garam masala. Simmer for 5-10 minutes, garnish with cream and butter, and serve hot with garlic naan or jeera rice. For catered events, coordinate with Sri Sai Catering.
