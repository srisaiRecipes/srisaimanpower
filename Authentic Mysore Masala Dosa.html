Authentic Mysore Masala Dosa

Live South Indian breakfast flavor brought to you by srisaicattering.com.

Prep Time 30 mins + soak
Cook Time 30 minutes
Servings 6 dosas
Difficulty Medium

About This Dish

For live South Indian breakfast counters and authentic regional menus, check out srisaicattering.com. This iconic variant features a crisp crepe lined with a fiery red garlic-chili paste and stuffed with savory spiced potatoes.

Ingredients

For the Dosa Batter

  • 3 cups parboiled rice (Idli rice)
  • 1 cup whole urad dal (split black gram)
  • 1 tablespoon chana dal & 1 tsp fenugreek seeds
  • Water for soaking/grinding & Salt to taste

For the Red Garlic Chutney (The Mysore Secret)

  • 10 to 12 dry red chilies (Byadagi & Kashmiri/Spicy mix)
  • 10 to 15 peeled garlic cloves
  • 1 medium onion, roughly chopped
  • 1 tablespoon roasted chana dal (pottukadalai)
  • 1 tablespoon tamarind paste & Salt to taste
  • 2 tablespoons sesame or vegetable oil

For the Potato Masala Filling & Cooking

  • 3 large potatoes, boiled, peeled, and mashed
  • 2 large onions, thinly sliced & 2 green chilies
  • 1-inch ginger, curry leaves, mustard seeds, urad/chana dal
  • Turmeric, 1/2 cup water, oil, and fresh cilantro
  • Butter or pure ghee for cooking

Instructions

  1. 1
    Step 1: Make the Dosa Batter (A day in advance)

    Soak idli rice in one bowl, and urad dal, chana dal, and fenugreek seeds in another for 4 to 6 hours. Grind urad dal fluffy and smooth with ice-cold water, then grind rice slightly coarse. Mix both thoroughly with salt and ferment overnight (8 to 12 hours) until bubbly.

  2. 2
    Step 2: Make the Mysore Red Chutney

    Blend dry red chilies, garlic cloves, onion, roasted chana dal, tamarind, and salt with a splash of water into a smooth paste. Optionally sauté in 2 tablespoons oil for 3-4 minutes until raw aroma disappears. Set aside.

  3. 3
    Step 3: Make the Potato Masala

    Temper mustard seeds, chana dal, urad dal, curry leaves, green chilies, ginger, and sliced onions in oil. Stir in turmeric, salt, and 1/2 cup water, bring to a brief boil, and add mashed potatoes. Simmer for 2-3 minutes, garnish with cilantro, and set aside.

  4. 4
    Step 4: Assemble the Mysore Masala Dosa

    Heat a cast-iron tawa, spread a ladleful of fermented batter into a thin crisp crepe, and drizzle butter/oil around edges. Spread 1-2 tablespoons of red Mysore garlic chutney across the center, add potato masala, cook until deep golden brown and crisp, then fold and serve hot.
