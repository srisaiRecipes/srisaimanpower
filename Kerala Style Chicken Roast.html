Kerala Style Chicken Roast

Rich, spicy traditional flavors brought to you by srisaicattering.com.

Prep Time 20 minutes
Cook Time 45 minutes
Servings 4 servings
Difficulty Medium

About This Dish

For rich, spicy traditional feast starters and expert event catering, check out srisaicattering.com. This semi-dry Kerala chicken roast features shallow-fried chicken coated in a heavily caramelized onion-tomato masala.

Ingredients

For Marinating and Frying the Chicken

  • 1.5 lbs (700g) chicken pieces (bite-sized)
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 tablespoon Kashmiri red chili powder
  • 1 tablespoon coriander powder
  • 1 tablespoon vinegar or lemon juice
  • Salt to taste & Coconut oil for frying

For the Roast Masala

  • 3 large onions, thinly sliced
  • 2 tablespoons shallots (small onions), sliced
  • 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 green chilies, slit & 2 sprigs fresh curry leaves
  • 2 medium tomatoes, finely chopped
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 tablespoon Kashmiri red chili powder
  • 1.5 tablespoons coriander powder
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • 1/2 cup water & Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. 1
    Marinate the Chicken

    In a mixing bowl, combine the chicken pieces with turmeric powder, red chili powder, coriander powder, vinegar (or lemon juice), and salt. Mix well and let it marinate for 20 to 30 minutes.

  2. 2
    Shallow Fry the Chicken

    Heat 3 tablespoons of coconut oil in a heavy-bottomed pan or skillet over medium-high heat. Place the marinated chicken pieces in a single layer and shallow fry them until golden brown and cooked halfway through (about 4 to 5 minutes per side). Remove the chicken and set aside. (Keep the leftover flavored oil in the pan).

  3. 3
    Sauté Aromatics

    In the same pan with the remaining oil, add the thinly sliced onions, shallots, slit green chilies, and a generous sprig of fresh curry leaves. Sauté on medium heat until the onions turn golden brown and caramelized (about 8 to 10 minutes).

  4. 4
    Add Ginger-Garlic Paste

    Stir in the ginger-garlic paste and sauté for 1 to 2 minutes until the raw aroma completely disappears.

  5. 5
    Add Spices and Tomatoes

    Add the chopped tomatoes and cook until they soften and mash into the onion base. Lower the heat and add the turmeric powder, Kashmiri red chili powder, and coriander powder. Sauté the spice mixture for 2 minutes until the oil begins to separate.

  6. 6
    Combine Chicken and Masala

    Add the fried chicken pieces into the pan, tossing well so the thick onion-tomato masala thoroughly coats every piece.

  7. 7
    Roast to Perfection

    Pour in 1/2 cup of water, add the garam masala, and mix well. Cover the pan with a lid and simmer on low heat for 15 to 20 minutes until the chicken is completely tender and the masala clings tightly to the meat.

  8. 8
    Final Dry Roast

    Remove the lid, increase the heat to medium, and roast the mixture while stirring constantly until the masala turns dark brown, semi-dry, and slightly crisp.

  9. 9
    Garnish and Serve

    Drizzle 1 tablespoon of raw coconut oil and toss in a fresh sprig of curry leaves right at the end. Turn off the heat and serve hot with appam, parotta, or steamed rice.
