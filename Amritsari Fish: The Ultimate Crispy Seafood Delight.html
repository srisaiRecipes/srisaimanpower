Amritsari Fish: The Ultimate Crispy Seafood Delight

Authentic Punjabi street-food feasts and party starters brought to you by srisaicattering.com.

Prep Time 20 minutes
Cook Time 15 minutes
Servings 4 servings
Difficulty Medium

What Makes It Special?

Unlike heavy English-style batters, Amritsari fish uses a light, spiced gram flour (besan) batter infused with carom seeds (ajwain), ginger-garlic paste, and lemon juice. The ajwain is crucial—it not only cuts through the richness of the fried fish but also aids digestion and imparts a distinct earthy aroma.

Authentic Recipe & Ingredients

Fish & First Marinade

  • 500g boneless firm white fish (Sole, Swai, Tilapia, or Cod), cut into finger strips
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice or vinegar
  • 1/2 tsp salt & 1/2 tsp Kashmiri red chili powder

The Batter & Frying

  • 1/2 cup gram flour (besan)
  • 2 tbsp rice flour or cornstarch (for extra crunch)
  • 1 large egg (optional, helps bind crispiness)
  • 1/2 tsp carom seeds (ajwain) & 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala & 1/2 tsp chaat masala
  • Water (for coating consistency) & Neutral cooking oil for frying

Step-by-Step Preparation

  1. 1
    First Marinade

    Wash and pat dry fish strips thoroughly with paper towels. In a bowl, mix fish with lemon juice, ginger-garlic paste, salt, and red chili powder. Let it rest for 15–20 minutes to draw out excess moisture and season the flesh.

  2. 2
    Prepare the Batter

    In a mixing bowl, combine besan, rice flour, ajwain, turmeric, garam masala, and chaat masala. Add the egg and a splash of water, whisking gradually until you achieve a thick, smooth batter that can coat the back of a spoon.

  3. 3
    Combine

    Transfer the marinated fish (drain any watery liquid released) directly into the batter. Mix gently so every piece is thoroughly coated.

  4. 4
    Deep Frying

    Heat oil in a heavy-bottomed kadhai or deep fryer over medium-high heat. Drop fish pieces one by one into the hot oil. Fry on medium heat for 4 to 5 minutes until the coating turns golden-brown and intensely crispy. Do not overcrowd; fry in batches.

  5. 5
    Drain & Serve

    Remove with a slotted spoon and drain on paper towels. Garnish with chaat masala and fresh coriander. Serve piping hot with mint-cilantro chutney and lemon-soaked onion rings. For larger parties, coordinate options with specialists like Sri Sai Catering.
