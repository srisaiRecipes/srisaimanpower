Authentic Odia Chhena Poda

Traditional Odia festival dessert spreads and unique regional sweets brought to you by srisaicattering.com.

Prep Time 20 minutes
Cook Time 45 - 60 mins
Servings 6 servings
Difficulty Medium

About This Dish

For traditional Odia festival dessert spreads, unique regional sweets, and authentic celebratory event catering, check out expert services like srisaicattering.com. Chhena Poda is Odisha's signature dessert, featuring fresh homemade cottage cheese kneaded with sugar and semolina, then slow-baked until deeply caramelized and rich.

Ingredients

Chhena, Sweeteners, & Aromatics

  • 2 liters full-fat/whole milk (for fresh chhena/paneer)
  • 3 tablespoons lemon juice or vinegar (for curdling)
  • 3/4 cup fine granulated sugar (or organic jaggery powder)
  • 2 tablespoons semolina (suji/rava) or rice flour
  • 2 tablespoons pure ghee & 3 to 4 tbsp reserved whey or milk
  • 1 teaspoon green cardamom powder
  • 2 tablespoons cashew nuts and raisins (lightly fried in ghee)
  • Fresh banana leaves (traditional pan lining, optional)

Instructions

  1. 1
    Step 1: Make Fresh Chhena (Cottage Cheese)

    Bring full-fat milk to a boil. Turn off heat, let it cool for 2 minutes, then slowly add lemon juice/vinegar, stirring until milk curdles and greenish whey separates. Strain through a muslin cloth, rinse briefly under cold water, and gently squeeze excess water while keeping chhena moist and soft (do not hang dry). Reserve 3 to 4 tablespoons of whey.

  2. 2
    Step 2: Knead the Chhena Mixture

    Transfer fresh chhena to a large bowl. Using the heel of your palm, knead continuously for 4 to 5 minutes until smooth, creamy, and non-grainy. Mix in sugar, semolina (or rice flour), cardamom powder, and a few tablespoons of reserved whey/milk until it resembles a moderately thick cake batter. Gently fold in fried cashews and raisins.

  3. 3
    Step 3: Prepare and Bake

    Preheat oven to 180°C (350°F). Line a cake pan with a clean banana leaf (for smoky aroma) and grease with pure ghee. Optional: sprinkle a tablespoon of sugar at the base for extra caramel crust. Pour batter into the pan, level the top, and bake for 40 to 45 minutes until the exterior turns deep, dark golden-brown (almost caramelized on edges). Test doneness with a toothpick.

  4. 4
    Step 4: Cool and Serve

    Allow the cake to cool completely in the pan so it firms up and settles. Invert onto a serving plate, cut into neat squares or wedges, and serve at room temperature or chilled.
