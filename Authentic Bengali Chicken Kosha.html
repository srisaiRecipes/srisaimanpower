Authentic Bengali Chicken Kosha (Kosha Mangsho Style)

Prep Time 30 mins + marinate
Cook Time 50 minutes
Servings 4 servings
Difficulty Medium

About This Dish

Chicken Kosha is an iconic slow-cooked preparation featuring deeply caramelized onions, rich mustard oil, and a concentrated semi-dry gravy that clings tightly to tender marinated chicken.

Chicken Kosha Ingredients

For the Chicken Marinade

  • 1.5 lbs (700g) bone-in chicken pieces (curry cut)
  • 1/2 cup plain yogurt (dahi), beaten smooth
  • 1 tbsp ginger paste & 1 tbsp garlic paste
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder & 1.5 tbsp mustard oil
  • Salt to taste

For the Kosha Gravy Base

  • 3 tablespoons mustard oil
  • Whole Spices: 2 green cardamoms, 4 cloves, 1-inch cinnamon, 2 bay leaves, 2 dry red chilies, 1/2 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 teaspoon sugar or jaggery (for caramelization)
  • 3 large onions, thinly sliced & 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste each
  • 2 medium tomatoes, finely chopped
  • 1.5 tbsp Kashmiri red chili powder, 1 tsp cumin, 1.5 tbsp coriander
  • 1/2 teaspoon warm Bengali Garam Masala powder
  • 1 cup warm water & fresh cilantro for garnish

Chicken Kosha Instructions

  1. 1
    Step 1: Marinate the Chicken

    Mix chicken pieces with yogurt, ginger paste, garlic paste, turmeric powder, mustard oil, and salt. Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour (2–3 hours for best results).

  2. 2
    Step 2: Caramelize and Build Aromatics

    Heat 3 tbsp mustard oil in a heavy kadai until smoking, then lower heat slightly. Add 1 tsp sugar to caramelize until deep amber-brown. Drop in whole spices (cardamoms, cloves, cinnamon, bay leaves, dry red chilies, cumin seeds) to sizzle for 10 seconds. Sauté sliced onions on medium-high until deeply golden brown and caramelized (10–12 mins).

  3. 3
    Step 3: Cook the Masala Base

    Stir in ginger and garlic pastes, cooking for 2 minutes. Add chopped tomatoes, Kashmiri red chili powder, cumin powder, and coriander powder. Sauté vigorously, splashing a bit of water if needed, until oil separates from the sides (about 8 minutes).

  4. 4
    Step 4: Kosha (Slow Sautéing Technique)

    Add marinated chicken with residual juices. Sauté and sear continuously in the thick masala over medium heat for 15 to 20 minutes without adding water (koshano) to concentrate flavors.

  5. 5
    Step 5: Simmer and Finish

    Pour in 1 cup warm water, cover tightly, and simmer on low for 15–20 minutes until tender. Sprinkle warm Bengali garam masala, stir, and rest covered for 10 minutes. Garnish with cilantro and serve with Luchi, Basmati rice, Pulao, or parathas. For catering spreads, coordinate with Sri Sai Catering.

Authentic Bengali Mishti Doi (Sweet Yogurt)

Prep Time 15 minutes
Cook Time 35 minutes
Servings 4 to 6 servings
Difficulty Medium

About This Dish

For traditional Bengali festival dessert spreads and authentic regional event catering, check out expert services like srisaicattering.com. Mishti Doi is a legendary Bengali delicacy made from reduced full-fat milk sweetened with caramelized sugar, fermented to perfection inside porous earthen pots.

Mishti Doi Ingredients

  • 1 liter full-fat/whole milk (crucial for rich texture)
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar (divided for caramelization and sweetness, or use 3/4 cup date palm jaggery / khejur gur)
  • 1/4 cup water (for making caramel)
  • 1/4 cup fresh, thick yogurt (curd) starter at room temperature
  • 1/4 teaspoon green cardamom powder (optional)
  • Earthen pots / terracotta bowls (traditional vessels)

Mishti Doi Instructions

  1. 1
    Step 1: Reduce the Milk

    Bring full-fat milk to a boil in a heavy-bottomed pan, stirring occasionally. Reduce heat to low and simmer until reduced to about two-thirds or half of its original volume, scraping down cream from the sides. Cool slightly.

  2. 2
    Step 2: Make the Caramel

    In a small saucepan, add 1/4 cup sugar and 1/4 cup water over low heat. Melt without constant stirring until it turns a deep amber-brown caramel color. Carefully pour into the warm reduced milk, stirring continuously to achieve a creamy beige-brown hue. Cool until lukewarm (40°C–45°C).

  3. 3
    Step 3: Incorporate the Culture

    Whisk 1/4 cup thick yogurt starter until smooth. Temper with a few spoonfuls of the lukewarm caramel-milk, then pour back into the main pot and whisk thoroughly until tiny bubbles form on top.

  4. 4
    Step 4: Set and Chill

    Pour mixture into clean earthen pots. Cover securely with lids or foil and place in a warm, undisturbed spot (like a turned-off microwave with light on) for 10 to 12 hours to ferment. Refrigerate for at least 2 hours before serving. Garnish with chopped pistachios or almonds. For traditional Bengali dessert catering, coordinate options with Sri Sai Catering.
