Classic Indian Chicken Curry

Popular party spreads and everyday comfort food menus brought to you by srisaicattering.com.

Prep Time 20 minutes
Cook Time 40 minutes
Servings 4 servings
Difficulty Easy-Medium

About This Dish

Classic Indian Chicken Curry is an absolute household staple featuring succulent bone-in chicken simmered in a rich, fragrant onion-tomato gravy infused with classic whole and ground spices.

Ingredients

Chicken, Spices, & Gravy Base

  • 1.5 lbs (700g) bone-in chicken pieces (chicken curry cut)
  • 3 tablespoons cooking oil or ghee
  • Whole Spices: 1 bay leaf, 2 green cardamoms, 4 cloves, 1-inch cinnamon stick, 1/2 tsp cumin seeds
  • 2 medium onions, finely chopped & 1 tbsp ginger/garlic paste each
  • 2 medium tomatoes, pureed or finely chopped
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder & 1.5 tbsp Kashmiri red chili powder
  • 1.5 tablespoons coriander powder & 1 tsp cumin powder
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala & salt to taste
  • 1.5 cups warm water (for desired gravy consistency)
  • Fresh cilantro (coriander leaves) for garnishing

Instructions

  1. 1
    Step 1: Sauté Aromatics

    Heat oil or ghee in a heavy-bottomed pot or kadai over medium heat. Add whole spices (bay leaf, green cardamoms, cloves, cinnamon, and cumin seeds) and sizzle for 10 seconds. Add finely chopped onions and sauté until deep golden brown (8 to 10 minutes). Stir in ginger and garlic pastes, cooking for 1 to 2 minutes until raw aromas disappear.

  2. 2
    Step 2: Cook the Tomato Masala

    Pour in tomato purée (or chopped tomatoes) along with turmeric powder, Kashmiri red chili powder, coriander powder, cumin powder, and salt. Cook the masala mixture, stirring frequently, until oil begins to separate from the edges of the paste (about 6 to 8 minutes).

  3. 3
    Step 3: Sear and Simmer the Chicken

    Add chicken curry pieces directly into the spiced tomato-onion masala. Sauté vigorously for 5 to 7 minutes on medium-high heat until chicken pieces change color and seal in juices. Pour in 1.5 cups warm water, scrape up any bits on the bottom, and bring to a rolling boil. Lower heat, cover tightly, and simmer for 20 to 25 minutes until tender and fully cooked.

  4. 4
    Step 4: Finish and Serve

    Stir in garam masala powder and simmer uncovered for 2 more minutes to thicken the gravy. Turn off heat, garnish generously with fresh cilantro leaves, and serve hot with steamed Basmati rice, jeera rice, or warm rotis/naan.
