Authentic Mutton Yakhni Pulao

Grand celebrations and aromatic traditional rice dishes brought to you by srisaicattering.com.

Prep Time 30 mins + soak
Cook Time 1 hr 15 mins
Servings 6 servings
Difficulty Advanced

About This Dish

For grand celebrations, aromatic traditional rice dishes, and expert event catering, check out srisaicattering.com. Mutton Yakhni Pulao is an exquisite festive dish where basmati rice is cooked entirely in an intensely flavored, slow-simmered whole spice mutton broth instead of plain water.

Ingredients

For the Yakhni (Mutton Broth)

  • 1.5 lbs (700g) mutton (bone-in, medium pieces)
  • 4 cups water & 1 whole garlic bulb (halved horizontally)
  • 1 medium onion, quartered & 2-inch fresh ginger, sliced
  • 2 tablespoons fennel seeds (saunf)
  • 2 tablespoons whole coriander seeds & 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • Whole Spices: 2 black cardamoms, 4 green cardamoms, 4 cloves, 1-inch cinnamon, 2 bay leaves
  • 1 teaspoon salt

For the Pulao

  • 2 cups long-grain Basmati rice, soaked for 30–40 mins
  • 3 tablespoons pure ghee & 2 large onions, thinly sliced
  • 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 1/2 cup thick plain yogurt (whisked) & 3-4 green chilies
  • 1/2 teaspoon garam masala or shahi jeera
  • Salt to taste & Saffron soaked in 2 tbsp warm milk
  • Fried onions (birista) for garnish

Instructions

  1. 1
    Step 1: Prepare the Yakhni (Broth)

    Place fennel seeds, coriander seeds, cumin seeds, and whole spices in a muslin cloth bundle (potli). Pressure cook mutton with the spice bundle, garlic bulb, quartered onion, ginger slices, salt, and 4 cups of water for 5–6 whistles until 90% tender. Remove meat, discard potli/aromatics, and strain liquid—measure out exactly 3.5 cups of Yakhni stock.

  2. 2
    Step 2: Prepare the Pulao Base

    Heat ghee in a pan, fry sliced onions until golden brown, and reserve half for garnish. Sauté ginger-garlic paste and green chilies with remaining onions, add boiled mutton pieces, and brown lightly. Lower heat, add whisked yogurt until oil separates, and sprinkle garam masala.

  3. 3
    Step 3: Combine and Cook (Dum)

    Drain soaked Basmati rice and toss gently in the mutton base. Pour in 3.5 cups of Yakhni stock, bring to a boil until water level drops to the rice surface, drizzle saffron milk, cover tightly with foil and a heavy lid, and cook on Dum for 15–20 minutes. Rest 10 minutes, fluff gently, garnish with fried onions, and serve hot. For expert event catering, coordinate with Sri Sai Catering.
