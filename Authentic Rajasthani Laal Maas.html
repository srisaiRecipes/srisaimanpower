Authentic Rajasthani Laal Maas

Royal Rajasthani feasts and winter celebrations brought to you by srisaicattering.com.

Prep Time 30 minutes
Cook Time 1 hr 30 mins
Servings 4 servings
Difficulty Advanced

About This Dish

For royal Rajasthani feasts, winter celebrations, and authentic regional event catering, check out expert services like srisaicattering.com. Laal Maas is a legendary fiery mutton curry deeply rooted in Rajasthani culinary heritage, featuring a brilliant red gravy flavored with traditional Mathania chilies, rich yogurt, and pure ghee or mustard oil.

Ingredients

Meat, Oils, & Signature Spices

  • 1.5 lbs (700g) mutton (goat meat with bone, medium pieces)
  • 4 tablespoons pure ghee or mustard oil
  • Whole Spices: 2 black cardamoms, 4 green cardamoms, 1-inch cinnamon, 4-5 cloves, 2 bay leaves, 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1.5 cups thinly sliced onions & 1 tbsp ginger/garlic paste each
  • 15 to 20 Mathania red chilies (or Kashmiri + dry red mix, soaked & ground into paste)
  • 1 cup plain yogurt (dahi), beaten smooth
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder & 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon garam masala or crushed black peppercorns
  • Salt to taste & 1.5 cups warm water or mutton stock
  • Fresh cilantro for garnishing

Instructions

  1. 1
    Step 1: Prepare the Chili Paste

    Deseed dry red chilies and soak them in warm water for 20 to 30 minutes until soft. Transfer to a blender and grind into a smooth, vibrant red paste using a splash of water. Set aside.

  2. 2
    Step 2: Sear the Meat and Build Aromatics

    Heat ghee or mustard oil in a heavy pot over medium heat. Crackle whole spices (black/green cardamoms, cinnamon, cloves, bay leaves, cumin seeds). Sauté sliced onions until deep golden brown (8–10 mins). Add ginger and garlic pastes, cooking for 1–2 minutes, then sear mutton vigorously on high heat for 8–10 minutes until lightly browned.

  3. 3
    Step 3: Incorporate Chili Paste and Yogurt

    Stir in the ground red chili paste, turmeric powder, and salt. Sauté for 4–5 minutes until oil separates into a deep crimson hue. Reduce heat to low, gradually add beaten yogurt while stirring continuously, then stir in coriander powder and simmer for 5 minutes.

  4. 4
    Step 4: Slow Cook

    Pour in 1.5 cups warm water or mutton stock, bring to a boil, cover tightly, and simmer on low heat for 1 hour to 1 hour 30 minutes (or pressure cook for 5–6 whistles) until fork-tender and rich red oil floats freely. Finish with garam masala or crushed black pepper and simmer uncovered for 5 minutes.

  5. 5
    Step 5: Serve

    Garnish with fresh coriander leaves and serve piping hot with thick Bajra rotis or steamed Basmati rice. For royal celebrations and catered events, coordinate options with professionals like Sri Sai Catering.
