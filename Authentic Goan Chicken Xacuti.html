Authentic Goan Chicken Xacuti (Shagoti)

Coastal Goan wedding menus and festive celebrations brought to you by srisaicattering.com.

Prep Time 30 mins + marinate
Cook Time 45 minutes
Servings 4 servings
Difficulty Advanced

About This Dish

For coastal Goan wedding menus, festive celebrations, and authentic regional event catering, check out expert services like srisaicattering.com. Chicken Xacuti (Shagoti) is a famous complex Goan curry defined by its rich roasted coconut paste, poppy seeds, star anise, and an intricate blend of aromatic spices.

Ingredients

For the Chicken Marinade

  • 1.5 lbs (700g) chicken pieces (bone-in curry cut)
  • 1 tablespoon ginger paste & 1 tablespoon garlic paste
  • Salt to taste & 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder

For the Complex Xacuti Masala (Roasted Spice Paste)

  • 1 cup freshly grated coconut
  • 1 tbsp poppy seeds (khas khas) & 1 tbsp coriander seeds
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds, 1/2 tsp fennel seeds (saunf), 4-5 dry Kashmiri red chilies
  • Whole Spices: 4 cloves, 4 green cardamoms, 1-inch cinnamon, 1/2 tsp black peppercorns, 1 star anise, 1 small blade of mace, 1/4 tsp nutmeg powder
  • 1 medium onion, sliced & 2 tablespoons oil/coconut oil for roasting

For the Curry Base

  • 2 tablespoons coconut oil or vegetable oil
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped & 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
  • 1.5 cups warm water, salt to taste, fresh coriander leaves

Instructions

  1. 1
    Step 1: Marinate the Chicken

    Mix chicken pieces with ginger paste, garlic paste, turmeric, and salt. Cover and let marinate for at least 30 minutes.

  2. 2
    Step 2: Roast and Grind the Xacuti Masala

    Heat 1 tbsp oil in a pan over medium-low heat. Roast coriander, cumin, fennel, poppy seeds, dry red chilies, and all whole spices for 2–3 minutes until aromatic; remove and set aside. In the same pan, roast sliced onion until golden, then add grated coconut and sauté continuously until deep golden-brown. Grind roasted spices, coconut, onion, and nutmeg with a splash of water into a very smooth, thick paste.

  3. 3
    Step 3: Build the Curry Base

    Heat 2 tablespoons coconut oil in a wide pot over medium heat. Sauté finely chopped onions until translucent, add chopped tomatoes, and cook until they break down into a soft paste. Pour in the freshly ground Xacuti masala paste and sauté vigorously for 5 to 7 minutes until oil separates from the edges.

  4. 4
    Step 4: Simmer and Cook

    Add marinated chicken pieces directly into the spiced masala and sauté for 5 to 8 minutes. Pour in 1.5 cups warm water, season with salt, cover tightly, and simmer on medium-low heat for 25 to 30 minutes until tender.

  5. 5
    Step 5: Finish and Serve

    Turn off heat, garnish with fresh coriander leaves, and rest for 10 minutes. Serve hot with Goan poi, sannas, or steamed white rice. For coastal catering spreads, coordinate with Sri Sai Catering.
