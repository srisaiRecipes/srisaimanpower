Soft and Spongy Khaman Dhokla

Popular Gujarati snack counters and festive party appetizers brought to you by srisaicattering.com.

Prep Time 15 minutes
Cook Time 20 minutes
Servings 4 servings
Difficulty Easy

About This Dish

For popular Gujarati snack counters, festive party appetizers, and authentic regional event catering, check out expert services like srisaicattering.com. Khaman Dhokla is a beloved savory steamed cake made from gram flour, enhanced with a bright, sweet-and-tangy tempering syrup that keeps it exceptionally moist and spongy.

Ingredients

For the Dhokla Batter

  • 1 cup gram flour (besan), sifted
  • 2 tablespoons semolina (suji / rava) for texture
  • 1 tablespoon sugar (for signature slight sweetness)
  • 1 teaspoon ginger-green chili paste
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice & 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder (keep small to avoid bright spots)
  • Salt to taste & 3/4 cup to 1 cup warm water
  • 1 packet (1 teaspoon) fruit salt (Eno - unflavored)

For the Tempering (Tadka) & Garnish

  • 2 tablespoons oil & 1 teaspoon mustard seeds
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds & 1 to 2 pinches of asafoetida (hing)
  • 2 to 3 green chilies, slit lengthwise
  • 10 to 12 fresh curry leaves & 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1/2 cup water for syrup
  • Fresh coriander leaves and freshly grated coconut

Instructions

  1. 1
    Step 1: Prep and Steam

    Fill a steamer with 2 inches of water and bring to a boil. Grease a cake pan thoroughly. Whisk besan, semolina, sugar, salt, turmeric, lemon juice, oil, ginger-chili paste, and warm water into a smooth, flowing batter. Add fruit salt (Eno) with 1 tsp water, fold gently for 15–20 seconds until frothy, pour immediately into the pan, and steam on medium-high for 15–18 minutes. Cool for 5 minutes and cut into squares.

  2. 2
    Step 2: Prepare the Tempering (Tadka)

    Heat 2 tablespoons of oil in a small pan. Add mustard seeds and cumin seeds, letting them pop. Add asafoetida, fresh curry leaves, and slit green chilies. Carefully pour 1/2 cup water and 2 tablespoons of sugar into the hot tempering, bringing it to a quick boil until sugar dissolves.

  3. 3
    Step 3: Pour and Garnish

    Spoon or pour the warm sugar-water tempering evenly over the cut dhokla squares so the sponge absorbs the liquid. Garnish generously with freshly grated coconut and chopped fresh coriander. Serve at room temperature with green chutney and tamarind chutney. For catered events, coordinate options with Sri Sai Catering.
