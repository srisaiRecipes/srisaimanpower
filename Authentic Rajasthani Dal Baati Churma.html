Authentic Rajasthani Dal Baati Churma

Royal Rajasthani wedding feasts and traditional festive spreads brought to you by srisaicattering.com.

Prep Time 40 minutes
Cook Time 1 hour
Servings 4 to 6 servings
Difficulty Advanced

About This Dish

For royal Rajasthani wedding feasts, traditional festive spreads, and authentic regional event catering, check out expert services like srisaicattering.com. Dal Baati Churma is the culinary crown jewel of Rajasthan, featuring crunchy baked wheat balls drenched in pure ghee, served alongside a rich five-lentil panchmel dal and sweet, crumbly churma.

Ingredients

For the Baati (Baked Wheat Balls)

  • 3 cups coarse whole wheat flour (lapsi atta or regular with 2 tbsp semolina)
  • 1/2 cup ghee (melted, for shortening/moiyan)
  • 1/2 tsp carom seeds (ajwain), 1/2 tsp salt, 1/4 tsp baking soda
  • Warm water or milk (for a stiff dough) & extra ghee for dipping

For the Panchmel Dal (Five-Lentil Curry)

  • 2 tbsp each of 5 lentils: yellow moong dal, tur dal, chana dal, masoor dal, black urad dal (soaked 30 mins)
  • 3 tablespoons ghee or oil & 1 tsp cumin seeds, 1/2 tsp mustard seeds, pinch of hing
  • 1 large onion, chopped & 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 medium tomatoes, chopped & 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1.5 tbsp red chili powder & 1 tbsp coriander powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala, 4.5 cups water, salt, cilantro

For the Churma (Sweet Crumbled Wheat)

  • 2 baatis (reserved before ghee dip)
  • 1/2 cup powdered sugar (or jaggery powder)
  • 1/4 cup chopped mixed dry nuts (almonds, cashews, pistachios)
  • 1/2 teaspoon cardamom powder & 2 tbsp warm ghee

Instructions

  1. 1
    Step 1: Make and Bake the Baatis

    Combine coarse wheat flour, semolina, salt, ajwain, and baking soda. Rub in 1/2 cup melted ghee until it resembles breadcrumbs, then knead into a very stiff, tight dough with warm water or milk. Rest for 20 mins. Shape into lemon-sized balls, press a thumb indentation in the center, and bake at 200°C (400°F) for 35–45 minutes until golden brown with cracks. While piping hot, submerge each baati in warm melted ghee for 1–2 minutes.

  2. 2
    Step 2: Prepare the Panchmel Dal

    Cook the soaked 5 lentils with 4.5 cups water, turmeric, and salt in a pressure cooker for 5 to 6 whistles until soft and smooth; whisk lightly. Temper ghee/oil with cumin, mustard, and hing, sauté chopped onions until golden, add ginger-garlic paste, tomatoes, red chili powder, coriander powder, and salt until oil separates. Pour boiled dal into the masala, simmer for 10–15 minutes, stir in garam masala, and garnish with cilantro.

  3. 3
    Step 3: Make the Churma & Serve

    Break 2 warm baatis and pulse into a coarse, crumbly powder. Mix with powdered sugar (or jaggery powder), cardamom powder, chopped nuts, and 2 tablespoons warm ghee until well combined. Serve piping hot Panchmel Dal with ghee-drenched Baatis and sweet Churma. For professional catering options, coordinate with Sri Sai Catering.
